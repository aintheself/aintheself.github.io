Organizers

Workshop organizers

The organizers span machine learning, NLP, HCI, philosophy, education, developmental medicine, and culturally grounded approaches to AI.

Team

Interdisciplinary leadership

The workshop is organized across academia, industry research, and applied practice, with strengths in trustworthy AI, social and cultural aspects of technology, philosophy of AI, and developmental perspectives.

AA

Akhil Arora

Aarhus University

Professor of computer science leading the CLAN for AI Research on Language and Networks, with work in human-centered and trustworthy AI, multilinguality, culturally grounded language technologies, and reliable LLM inference.

Full bio

He is a fellow of the Copenhagen Center for Social Data Science, an affiliate of ELLIS Unit Copenhagen and the Pioneer Centre for AI, and a formal collaborator of the Wikimedia Foundation. His work has appeared at ICML, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, and WWW, and he has co-organized workshops at ACL, EMNLP, and SIGMOD.

MC

Monojit Choudhury

MBZUAI

Professor of Natural Language Processing and Chief Scientist of the Institute of Agriculture and AI at MBZUAI, working at the intersection of NLP, social and cultural aspects of technology use, and ethics.

Full bio

Previously a Principal Scientist at Microsoft Research Lab and Microsoft Turing India, he is also a member of the ACL Ethics Committee and has organized numerous workshops at major ACL venues, including TextGraphs and the Computational Processing of Code-mixing series.

MC

Mukund Choudhary

MBZUAI

PhD candidate researching how LLMs structure language, with current work on comparing human and model linguistic behavior and learning dynamics.

Full bio

He has regularly reviewed and volunteered at major ACL conferences and has also led organization, coordination, and outreach for the Panini Linguistics Olympiad for high-school students in India.

AJ

Abdoul Jalil Djiberou Mahamadou

MBZUAI

Postdoctoral Associate working on personalization, cultural sensitivity, and inclusion in AI, with previous work in healthcare ethics, bias and fairness, participatory AI, neuroscience, causal inference, and uncertainty.

Full bio

He is also part of the organizing committee for an incoming AACL and EMNLP tutorial on ethics in NLP.

JO

Jacki O’Neill

Microsoft Research Africa, Nairobi

Director and founding Lab Director of Microsoft Research Africa, Nairobi, applying ethnographic and HCI methods to build culturally grounded, human-centered AI across work, health, and agriculture.

Full bio

She received the ACM SIGCHI Societal Impact Award in 2026 and has served on organizing and program committees for CHI, CSCW, ICTD, and ECSCW, including co-organizing the Nairobi workshop behind the AI and the Future of Work in Africa white paper.

HP

Dr. Haneesha Pinnamaraju

SHINE Child Development Center, Dubai

Medical Director and Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrician whose work on neurodiversity and inclusive education brings developmental perspectives to the design and societal impact of AI.

Full bio

Her perspective centers on inclusion, human flourishing, and the diverse developmental needs of children and adolescents in AI-mediated settings.

SS

Sandrine R. Schiller

University of Copenhagen

Postdoctoral fellow at the Center for Philosophy of AI, studying intimate relationships with AI chatbots, generative AI and selfhood, mechanistic interpretability, and engagement optimization.

Full bio

Her research has appeared in AAAI/ACM venues, NAACL, and philosophy outlets, bridging philosophical inquiry with contemporary AI research.
