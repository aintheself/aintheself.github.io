Call for Papers

AI and the Self: Human Identity, Authenticity, and Agency in the Age of AI

We invite submissions on how AI systems shape selfhood, identity, authenticity, agency, reflection, dependence, culture, and authorship. The workshop welcomes technical, empirical, conceptual, and application-oriented perspectives.

Topics

Topics of interest

Measurement and evaluation

Methods for studying AI’s effects on self-concept, agency, confidence, dependence, identity formation, and skill change.

Technical design and interaction

Memory, personalization, uncertainty, abstention, refusal, and interaction design that avoid over-reliance or deceptive behavior.

Culture, language, and worldview

How AI systems encode assumptions about the self, intelligence, emotion, and agency across cultures and languages.

Education, work, and expertise

How AI changes learning, authorship, expertise, professional identity, and the meaning of human competence.

Ethical, legal, and philosophical foundations

Autonomy, responsibility, consciousness, personhood, and related questions raised by AI-mediated selfhood.

Application-oriented work

Submissions in education, healthcare, mental health, design, and related domains, including work in progress and demos.

Reviewers

Call for reviewers

We are recruiting reviewers—advanced PhD students, postdocs, faculty, and independent researchers— with backgrounds in AI/ML, NLP, HCI, cognitive science, psychology, philosophy of mind, sociology/anthropology, education, digital mental health/healthcare, ethics, law/policy, and related fields.

  • We especially welcome reviewers who are comfortable with interdisciplinary submissions.
  • Even if your expertise is rooted in one discipline or method, your perspective would be highly valuable.
  • Reviewers will typically be assigned 2–3 papers.
  • You will need an up-to-date OpenReview profile.
